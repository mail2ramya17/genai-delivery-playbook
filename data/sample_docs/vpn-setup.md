id: KB-101
title: VPN setup for remote employees
groups: [all-employees]
classification: internal
---
To connect to the corporate VPN, install the GlobalConnect client from the Software Center.
Sign in with your corporate email address and approve the MFA prompt on your phone.
If the client shows "gateway unreachable", switch networks and retry before opening a ticket.
VPN sessions disconnect automatically after 12 hours and require a fresh sign-in.
