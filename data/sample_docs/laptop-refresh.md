title: Laptop refresh policy
groups: [all-employees]
classification: internal
---
Laptops are refreshed every 4 years or sooner if the device fails a hardware diagnostic.
To request a replacement laptop, open a hardware request in the service portal.
Your old laptop must be returned within 10 business days so the disk can be wiped.
Engineering roles may request a 64 GB memory configuration with manager approval.
