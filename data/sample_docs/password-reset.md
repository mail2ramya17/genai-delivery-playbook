id: KB-102
title: Password reset and account lockout
groups: [all-employees]
classification: internal
---
You can reset your password yourself at the self-service password portal.
Accounts lock after 5 failed sign-in attempts and unlock automatically after 30 minutes.
The service desk will never ask for your password over chat, phone or email.
New passwords must be at least 14 characters and cannot reuse your last 10 passwords.
